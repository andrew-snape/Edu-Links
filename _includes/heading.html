{%- comment -%} Turns a snake_case sub-area key into a display heading and assigns it to the `heading` variable for the caller to use. Words listed under `acronyms` in _config.yml keep their fixed casing. The match is on the whole word, so "atl" becomes "ATL" while "atlas" stays "Atlas". Usage: assign the sub-area key to `key`, include this file, then output the `heading` variable it sets. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign words = include.key | replace: "_", " " | split: " " -%} {%- capture heading -%} {%- for word in words -%} {%- assign upper = word | upcase -%} {%- if site.acronyms contains upper %}{{ upper }} {% else %}{{ word | capitalize }} {% endif -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- endcapture -%} {%- assign heading = heading | strip -%}
